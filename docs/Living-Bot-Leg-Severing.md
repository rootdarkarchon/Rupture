# Living bot leg severing

Implemented in the active Rupture 1.2.1 source, impact protocol 14. The former `live_severing` branch name is historical provenance; the active directory has no Git history yet. Build inputs are installed SPT 4.1.6 / EFT 0.16.9.40743 and pinned Fika Core 2.4.4. This implementation has automated validation; live EFT and Fika acceptance is still outstanding.

## Gameplay contract

- Only living humanoid AI bots on the solo instance or Fika authority are eligible. Human players, human-controlled player scavs, simplified skeletons and downed actors are always excluded. By default only `assault`, `marksman`, `cursedAssault`, `assaultGroup`, `pmcBEAR` and `pmcUSEC` roles are eligible. **Living Leg Severing For Bosses And Special AI** is host synchronized and off by default; enabling it admits bosses, followers, raiders (`pmcBot`), rogues (`exUsec`), cultists and other special roles subject to the same AI/humanoid gates. Missing role metadata is rejected, and unknown roles are protected unless the special-AI checkbox is explicitly enabled. Role filtering applies to buildup and is rechecked before committing a queued cut. It does not restrict corpse dismemberment or reverse committed injuries.
- Direct damaging bullet leg hits use independent per-leg accumulated projectile momentum and squared probability rolls. **Living Leg Severing Momentum Threshold (N s)** defaults to 24, twice the separate 12 N s corpse default. Only shots hitting a leg already at zero HP add living buildup and can attempt severing. Healthy-leg hits and the hit that first blacks a leg add no living momentum. The first distinct hit after blackening starts that leg's living buildup from zero. Ignored projectiles are remembered so repeated callbacks cannot count the blackening shot later. Any HP recovery, including partial healing and passive regeneration, successful surgery/full restoration, or completed bleeding/fracture treatment on that leg clears living buildup and invalidates pending sever reservations. Condition treatment requires an existing treatable condition, sufficient item resource and uninterrupted completion; ineffective or interrupted use does not count. Projectile identities remain remembered after healing. Corpse buildup and corpse explosion behavior remain unchanged.
- **Living Leg Severing From Explosions Only** is host synchronized and off by default. Enabling it prevents bullet buildup and bullet cuts, including a queued bullet cut whose commit has not occurred. Explosions use the same rules regardless of this checkbox: no momentum accumulation, no blacked-leg requirement, one roll per leg per blast, and normal bot-role/master-switch eligibility.
- Living explosion chance uses distance `d` from the explosion center to the nearest point along the leg's thigh/calf segments, native damage radius `R`, applied health damage `D` and leg maximum HP `H`. For a positive damaging leg hit inside `R`, `d <= 0.5 m` guarantees selection. Beyond 0.5 m, chance is `((R - d) / (R - 0.5))^3 * min(1, D / H)`. At/outside the radius it is zero. A midpoint hit at full leg HP worth of damage has 12.5% chance; half that damage has 6.25%. Zero/blocked damage cannot sever, including within the guaranteed zone. `D` is EFT's actual health-loss result from the hit on that leg, including native blacked-limb redistribution; incoming explosive strength is not substituted. Native LOS/cover and health modifiers run before this decision. The bot must still be alive when admission and commit occur.
- The shared native `ExplosionSharedMethods.Explosion` hook captures the actual explosion center and `IExplosiveItem.MaxExplosionDistance`, including native calls outside the hand-grenade wrapper. Nested blast scopes restore outer identities without changing lethal-corpse suppression. A bounded 15-second, 128-blast registry resolves delayed fragments by exact center and source item when available. Unidentified or ambiguous fragments fail closed. Per-bot deduplication allows at most one roll per leg per blast, including failed rolls. Healing invalidates pending geometry reservations for both bullet and explosion cuts; it does not grant another roll for a previously processed blast. Corpse distance rules remain independent.
- Geometry and physics must be admitted before local health is changed. A failed cut retains the intact limb and health. Headless authority uses the same eligibility and temporary physics admission, without graphical mesh work.
- The fall lasts one second, followed by a 0.2-second blend to prone near the final pelvis position. Recovery uses nearby ground/navigation and native teleport to reset movement height/flying history; missing navigation does not return the bot to the shot location. Temporary bodies use the existing external scene and step owner. Detached limbs are captured with gravity and CCD enabled before their Unity shells are parked. No death, corpse object, Fika downing or corpse-drag API is fabricated. Active firing is stopped during the fall. Animators, character controller, native ragdoll resources and movement pause are restored afterward.
- Severed bots remain prone for their lifetime and can crawl, aim, shoot and heal. Standing, crouching, sprinting, jumping and vaulting are blocked. Surgery does not restore anatomy or mobility.
- After a cut commits, missing leg segments are removed from the temporary external ragdoll, including their mass, collisions, internal joints and the joint attaching them to the retained body. Below-knee cuts retain the thigh; above-knee cuts remove the thigh, calf and foot. Later falls omit those external bodies and joints from construction. All native shells remain parked until cleanup, and skeletal transforms remain available to skinning and later corpse construction. Living hit colliders are resolved directly from the player bones without requiring a corpse cache. Remote presentation reconciles its bone list with every authority snapshot, including a second cut during the same fall.
- Each untreated leg remains at zero current HP and native destroyed state. Rupture owns a separate persistent severed condition rather than inserting an unsupported effect type into EFT's fixed health-effect registries. Native light/heavy bleeding, residual wounds and fractures on that limb are removed.
- Each untreated stump applies four times the native heavy-bleeding health damage at the native interval, including the native dehydration variant. Energy loss uses the native heavy-bleed baseline per stump. Native damage modifiers remain in effect. A stalled frame cannot cause an unbounded catch-up burst. Visual blood uses shared budgets and can be suppressed independently of health loss.
- Only an uninterrupted native surgical item completion for the same health controller and leg can treat it. Native animation duration, successful restoration events and item cost are retained. Direct/full restore calls from other systems cannot bypass treatment. The result is one current HP and one maximum HP, including surgery skill bonuses. Treated stumps are excluded from future surgical target selection.
- Later stump damage follows normal damage and overdamage rules without reopening the severed bleeding condition. Ordinary healing cannot raise the stump above one HP. Missing legs persist into corpse visuals.
- The host-synchronized **Living Bot Leg Severing** setting defaults to enabled and also requires **Dismemberment Enabled**. Disabling prevents new severing; existing injuries, bleeding and treatment remain active.

## Ownership and replication

`BotSeverStateService` owns per-actor anatomy, treatment, damage timing and pending cut reservations. `BotSeverHooks` gates surgery and movement. `ShadowRagdollWorld.Living` owns temporary living rigs and detached-limb physics. Stump streams remain local presentation.

The host/headless sends reliable injury, treatment and phase transitions, transient pose samples at up to 20 Hz while falling, and periodic reliable current state while prone. Packets bind profile plus network ID to the current server session and reject invalid masks, nonfinite values, excessive arrays and stale revisions/pose sequences. Clients present trusted authority state without applying health damage. A bounded five-second queue handles delayed observed-player creation, and periodic state allows later retries of missing local geometry. Remote bots do not have to report local `IsAI`; the host made that eligibility decision.

Death releases living physics before native corpse construction. Despawn, health-controller replacement, backend failure, disconnect and raid reset retire temporary resources. Native corpse control ABI V1 remains for corpses only.

The terminal death hook immediately cancels active and queued uncommitted living cuts, releasing their reservations and async gib ownership. Generated gib renderers stay hidden until the body mesh commit succeeds. Committed anatomy and disabled hit colliders cross into the corpse handoff. Disabled colliders resolve body ownership from their retained hierarchy even when Unity no longer reports `attachedRigidbody`. Known missing corpse segments retain core pose indices as shape-free kinematic external actors, with no joint connections or dynamic mass contribution; an intact body with no colliders still fails admission. When death occurs during the one-second fall, EFT reuses the parked native shells and joints at the last completed physics pose rather than destroying and recreating pooled components synchronously while Unity destruction is deferred.
